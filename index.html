Speed = Target time ÷ Rendered time (above 100% = faster than target). Productivity = Rendered time ÷ available working hours (6.5 hrs × days worked). Overall = Speed × Productivity. Colors: green ≥ 100%, amber 80–99%, red < 80%. Only finished pickings count; breaks are already removed from rendered time, and overlapping pickings by the same picker on the same day are counted once. A day counts as worked if the picker finished at least one picking that day. Click a row to see the day-by-day breakdown.
